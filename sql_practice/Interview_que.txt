
# ✅ Table & Sample Data

```sql
CREATE TABLE employees (
    emp_id INT PRIMARY KEY,
    emp_name VARCHAR(100),
    manager_id INT,
    join_date DATE,
    salary DECIMAL(10,2),
    department VARCHAR(50),
    FOREIGN KEY (manager_id) REFERENCES employees(emp_id)
);
```

# 🔥 Practice Question Bank

### A. Self-Join Based

1. Find employees who joined **before their managers**.
2. List managers who have at least one report that joined earlier.
3. Find employees who have the **same manager**.
4. Find pairs of employees where **one earns more than the other** but both are in the same department.
5. Find employees whose **manager is in a different department**.

---

### B. Aggregation / Group By

6. Find the **average salary per department**.
7. Find departments with **more than 2 employees**.
8. Find the **highest-paid employee in each department**.
9. Find managers who manage **more than 2 employees**.
10. Find the department with the **earliest joiner**.
 
---

### C. Nested / Correlated Subqueries

11. Find employees who earn **more than the average salary** of their department.
12. Find employees who joined **before the earliest join date of their manager’s department**.
13. Find employees with salary **greater than their manager’s salary**.
14. Find the **second highest salary** in the company.
15. Find employees who do **not manage anyone**.

---

### D. Advanced Joins & Conditions

16. Find all **managers and their direct reports** (manager name + employee name).
17. Find employees who **joined in the same year** as their manager.
18. Find employees whose **salary is within 10% of their manager’s salary**.
19. Find employees in **departments where average salary > 70k**.
20. Find managers who manage employees **from more than one department**.
